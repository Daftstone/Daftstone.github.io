Trustworthy machine learning · Data mining

Chenwang Wu

Postdoctoral Researcher / Hong Kong Baptist University

My research focuses on trustworthy machine learning and data mining, with work on machine-generated text detection, robust recommender systems, and model interpretation.

I am a postdoc at Hong Kong Baptist University, working with Yiu-ming Cheung and Bo Han. I received my Ph.D. from the School of Data Science at the University of Science and Technology of China in 2024, advised by Defu Lian, and my B.S. from Fuzhou University in 2018.
